# Changelog

## 0.1.0 – 2026-10-04

- First version: see at a glance what's in which drawer, how much, and since when.
- Add food with suggestions from earlier entries; today's date is filled in automatically.
- Take out part or all of an entry with digit wheels, plus/minus buttons or by typing the amount.
- Copy the label text (name, amount and date) to paste into a label printer app.
- Drawers, optional drawer groups and units can be set up in Settings.
- Language and theme can be chosen per person; English and German.

*Deutsch:* Erste Version: Übersicht nach Schubladen, Einfrieren mit Vorschlägen und
automatischem Datum, Entnehmen per Zahlenrädchen, Plus/Minus oder Eintippen,
Etikettentext kopieren, Schubladen, Gruppen und Einheiten einstellbar, Sprache und Design
pro Person.
